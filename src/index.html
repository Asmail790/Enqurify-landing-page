AI CUSTOMER SUPPORT FOR BUSINESSES

Customer support that answers every call, chat and enquiry 24 / 7

Enquirify is AI customer support for businesses of every size. It answers phone calls, website chats and enquiry forms the moment they arrive, qualifies each customer with the details you actually need, and turns the conversation into a confirmed booking — so nothing waits in a queue, nothing goes to voicemail, and your business keeps selling long after the doors have closed.

ENQUIRIFY FORMS

Website forms and live chat that capture every enquiry, qualify the customer and hand back a booked order — every hour of the day.

It asks for the name, number, address and preferred time, checks the details against your availability, and writes the job into your calendar before the customer has even closed the tab.

ENQUIRIFY VOICE

A phone assistant that answers every call, takes the order and writes the booking straight into your calendar, with a full transcript attached.

It speaks naturally, copes with interruptions and never rushes a caller. Anything urgent is transferred to your mobile with the whole conversation attached.

ENQUIRIFY EMAIL

Replies to your inbox in seconds, drafts the follow-up itself and keeps every thread logged in one shared place your team can pick up.

Quotes, prices and availability come from the answers you have already approved, so nothing is invented and no enquiry is left half-answered overnight.

ENQUIRIFY API

Drop the same engine into your own product with a REST API, webhooks and full control over every conversation it handles.

Send an enquiry in and get back a qualified lead, a booking or a full transcript — then push it wherever your team already works.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What businesses usually ask before they switch.

How quickly can we go live?

Most businesses are live within a day. We connect your existing number and website forms, train the assistant on your services and prices, then test it with you before switching on.

Do we need new phone hardware?

No. Enquirify works with the number you already advertise. If you would rather have desk phones, we can supply and install them free in London and Greater London.

Will customers know they are talking to AI?

We are upfront about it. The assistant introduces itself as Enquirify, answers naturally, and hands the conversation to your team if the customer asks for a person.

What happens when the AI cannot answer?

It captures the details, tells the customer when to expect a reply, and routes the enquiry to the right person with the full transcript attached.

Which tools and calendars can it connect to?

Google Calendar, Microsoft 365, Shopify, Square, Stripe, HubSpot, Slack and Zapier out of the box. Xero, QuickBooks, WooCommerce and internal systems can be connected as custom work.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No setup fee and no lock-in. Plans are billed monthly and you can cancel any time. Consultation and installation are included, and prices exclude VAT.
