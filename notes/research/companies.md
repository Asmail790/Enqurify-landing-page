# Categorized AI Communication & Voice Automation Companies

This document categorizes **10 AI communication and voice automation providers** based on company size, target market, and operational scale.

---

## Size Category Definitions

To evaluate these providers, company size is defined by **headcount, capital raised, customer tier, and market reach**:

* **Large / Enterprise:**
  * **Criteria:** 100+ employees, $50M–$100M+ in funding or public backing, global scale, and focused on serving Fortune 500 or multi-national enterprises with high security and custom contracts.
* **Medium / High-Growth Scale-Ups:**
  * **Criteria:** 20–100 employees, Series A/B funded ($5M–$50M raised), developer- or agency-focused infrastructure, and built for mid-market teams or growing business clients.
* **Small / Micro-SaaS & Bootstrapped SMB Tools:**
  * **Criteria:** 1–20 employees, lean or self-funded teams, lightweight self-serve software, designed specifically for low-complexity local businesses, sole traders, or small shops.
