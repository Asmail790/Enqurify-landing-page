# Goal

Recreate the landing page. Make it look however you want — give it your own look — but include the general sections.

# The product — Enquirify

Enquirify is a B2B product that provides AI automation for its customers through phone calls, website communication or any other form of general communication. The company using it then needs no support staff and can stay available 24/7.



# Enquirify landing page — design notes

Why each part of the page exists, in page order.

---

## General

- Changed the colour palette to black and orange (dark mode) so the page stands out.
- Gave all cards less border-radius for a harder, more industrial paper feel.
- Removed the wavy lines, since they are not associated with streamlining things.
- Added a small divider (white line) between sections.
- Added a dark/light mode button.
- Designed around cards, because they are simple to maintain.
- The text in the top section and the LIVE DEMO section is too long and in practice should be shortened. Not done yet.
- Creating semantic tokens (tokens with semantic meaning, like `color-danger` or `color-success`) for consistent manipulation of the design.

## Hero (top section)

- Included only the most important buttons at the top: "Get started" (straight to the pricing section), "Live demo" (for visitors who are not convinced and want to try it out on the site) and "Reviews" (what other people say about the product).
- Added a video section and gallery at the front, so every product can be explained with a short video showing how it works. It is also easy to add new products. The carousel should wrap around, but that can be done later. The text underneath should be short (a bullet list, or one or two short sentences) to explain the essence of the product.

## Our platform

- Show a big dashboard so visitors get an idea, or a glimpse, of how it works and feels. For optimal performance it should be an image, but for now it is HTML.

## Enquirify Forms and Enquirify Voice

- For both "ENQUIRIFY FORMS" and "ENQUIRIFY VOICE", added steps that explain how the product works, in simple steps (x, y, z).
- Also added cards that explain additional features (each with a form icon, title and description, so the reader can easily see what it does).
- Kept your initial "product view", but made it animated to illustrate how it is expected to work.

## Guardrails

- Added a guardrails section. Many companies worry about the backlash of saying something wrong — what happens if it classifies wrongly, or says something that is not true.

## Smart features

- Explain why it is better than the others (because it is more thoroughly thought out, and not just a voice and chat bot).

## Live demo

- Added a live demo for both products (Enquirify Forms and Enquirify Voice) so visitors can test the product directly. Currently it only responds with the same text.
- If the visitor is convinced, they can jump straight to the pricing section; if they want a longer, more realistic trial, they can jump to the product trial section.

## Reviews

- Show reviews from users known at companies, so visitors are assured the product works as expected. Good to have in future, once there are some customers.
- The stars are not that important; only what they say.

## The numbers

- Show a quantitative price reduction from using your service. The numbers are animated for a better effect. Good to have in future, once there are some customers.

## Integrations

- Many people are interested in how the service can integrate with their current platform. Having this page reassures them that Enquirify can easily integrate with others.

## FAQ

- Included an FAQ section for any remaining doubts, with email links so visitors can email support.

## Book a trial

- Has a dedicated form, so visitors can send straight from the website (no mailto link that needs an email client).
